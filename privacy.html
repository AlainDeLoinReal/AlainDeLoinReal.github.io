Privacy Policy — rclone MF5790

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Purpose

rclone MF5790 is a personal OAuth client used by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive account through rclone.

Google user data

The application may access Google Drive files only when authorized by the account owner and only for personal file management performed through rclone.

Data storage

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer by rclone. This website does not collect or store Google Drive content.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or disclosed to third parties by this application.

Data retention

The application does not maintain a separate server-side database of Google user data. Locally stored rclone configuration can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the security settings of the associated Google account.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
